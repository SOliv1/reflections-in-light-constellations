SHARE LOCATIONS EXPLAINED:

1️⃣  "🔗 SHARE COLLECTION" (Top Right)
    └─ Shares ALL photos in this album/gallery
    └─ Opens ShareModal for the collection
    └─ Creates one link for entire album

2️⃣  "🔗" (Photo Tile Hover)
    └─ Appears when you hover over a photo in the grid
    └─ Shares just that ONE photo
    └─ Opens ShareModal for single photo

STEP-BY-STEP WALKTHROUGH:

Step 1: NAVIGATE TO GALLERY
       ─────────────────────
       Open your Gallery or Day view
       You'll see a grid of photos

Step 2: FIND THE SHARE BUTTONS
       ──────────────────────────
       Option A) Look at the TOP RIGHT
                 You'll see: "🔗 Share Collection"
       
       Option B) HOVER over any photo in the grid
                 You'll see: [🔗] [❤️] [🗑️]

Step 3: CLICK A SHARE BUTTON
       ──────────────────────
       🔗 Share Collection  → Shares all photos in album
       🔗 (on photo)        → Shares just that photo

Step 4: SHAREMODAL OPENS
       ───────────────────
       You see the share options popup

Step 5: CONFIGURE SHARE
       ─────────────────
       Choose: Public / Friends / Password
       Set: Permissions, Mood, Caption

Step 6: CREATE LINK
       ──────────────
       Click "Create share link" button

Step 7: LINK CREATED
       ──────────────
       Backend generates: http://localhost:3000/share/soft-dawn-7f3k
       Copy and share with friends!

🔗  = Share button (creates shareable link)

COMMON QUESTIONS:

Q: Where is the "Share" button?
A: Hover over a photo in the gallery grid, look at the bottom-right corner

Q: Can I share an entire album?
A: Yes! Click "🔗 Share Collection" at the top of the gallery

Q: What happens after I click Share?
A: A modal pops up where you choose how to share (public/friends/password)

Q: How do I get the share link?
A: After clicking "Create share link", the URL is generated:
   Example: http://localhost:3000/share/soft-dawn-7f3k

Q: Can recipients download the photo?
A: Yes, but only if you enable it in the Permissions section

Q: Can I password-protect a share?
A: Yes! Select "Password protected" in the share modal

Q: Can I see who viewed my shared photo?
A: Yes! View analytics show viewer emails and counts

================================================================================

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS (Optional to add):

Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + S    = Open share modal for selected photo
Cmd/Ctrl + C            = Copy share link to clipboard
